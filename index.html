01 — About PrimePath RCM

Built for the practices that don't have time to chase their own money.

Our Purpose

Healthcare providers exist to treat patients — not to spend their evenings decoding payer rejection codes. PrimePath RCM exists to close that gap. Our purpose is simple: take full ownership of the billing cycle — from eligibility checks and clean claim submission through denial management and payment posting — so every dollar a practice earns actually reaches its account, on time and in full.

We were founded on a frustration shared by nearly every independent clinic, specialty practice, and solo provider we've spoken with: billing errors and slow reimbursements quietly bleed revenue that never gets noticed until it's a real problem. PrimePath RCM was built to be the fix — a dedicated, accountable billing partner that treats a client's cash flow with the same urgency as its own.

Introduction

PrimePath RCM is a Miami, Florida–based medical billing and revenue cycle management company serving physicians, clinics, and specialty healthcare providers across the United States. We combine certified billing expertise, payer-specific claim scrubbing, and transparent reporting into one straightforward service — with no hidden fees and no long-term lock-in.

From patient intake to final reimbursement, our team manages the entire revenue cycle: insurance verification, coding accuracy review, claims submission, rejection and denial follow-up, patient statements, and monthly financial reporting. Practices that work with us typically see faster turnaround on claims, fewer denials, and a clearer picture of exactly where their revenue stands at any moment.
